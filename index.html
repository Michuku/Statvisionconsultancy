About Us

Data-Driven Insights, Grounded in Real Experience

StatVision Research & Consultancy is a Nairobi-based data analysis and research firm helping organizations turn raw data into decisions they can act on. We combine statistical expertise in SPSS, Stata, R, and Python with hands-on experience in monitoring & evaluation, business intelligence, and research consultancy.

From government agencies and NGOs to private companies and academic institutions, our clients trust us to bring analytical rigor, clear reporting, and practical recommendations to every engagement.
